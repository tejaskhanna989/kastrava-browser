Privacy by design

Private by construction.

Not a mode you switch on. The architecture itself — every claim verifiable in the public source.

Memory-only sessions

Cache, cookies and site data never touch your disk. Quit — or crash — and it's gone. Your downloads stay yours.

Tracker radar

Live per-tab count of third-party requests and cookies, with strict blocking, DNT and no referrers.

Independent search

KastravaSearch by default. One click to DuckDuckGo, Brave, Google or Ecosia — no tracking either way.

A real browser

Tabs, workspaces, split view, vertical tabs, video pop-out, screenshots, full keyboard control and devtools.

Anti-fingerprinting

Randomised canvas, font and hardware signals so sites cannot pin a unique identity on your device.

Banking guard

Hardens known banking sites against trackers and script abuse — without breaking the pages you need.

Answers

Straight answers.

What disappears when I quit?
Everything sites stored: cache, cookies and site data. It lives in RAM and is wiped on exit. Downloads, settings and bookmarks stay — unless you switch those off too.
Is it really free?
Yes. The entire browser is free and open source, with no paid tier, no subscription and no account. Everything you see here is included in the download.
Do I need an account?
No. Kastrava needs no sign-up, no email and no profile. There is nothing to log in to — install it and start browsing.
Does it phone home?
No telemetry, no analytics, no activation calls. The browser works fully offline, and we cannot see what you do with it.
Why RC?
Release candidate — still being refined, so rough edges may exist. The privacy core is already working and the source is open for anyone to inspect.
Which platforms are supported?
Linux (Arch, Debian/Ubuntu, AppImage, Fedora), Windows (installer and portable) and Android (APK). No macOS build yet.
Is it Chromium-based?
Yes — the same engine as Chrome with our own browser built on top. None of Google accounts or tracking are included.
Where do I get help?
Open an issue on the GitHub repository, or email email-center@kastrava.in
